Tips for First International Trip
🚕
Bangkok Taxi
Always say "meter please." Use Grab app — set destination in app, shows fare upfront, no haggling.
💧
Water
Drink bottled water only. Never tap water anywhere in Thailand.
🛕
At Temples
Remove shoes. Women cover shoulders + knees. Never point feet at Buddha.
👶
Vivaan Near Water
Life jacket always on near sea. Never near monkeys. Thailand beaches have strong waves.
💳
Paying
Cards work at malls + big restaurants. Small stalls and street food = cash only.
🤝
Tipping
Not mandatory. 20–50 THB at restaurants. 100 THB for tour guides. Always appreciated.
📱
Google Translate
Camera feature — point at any Thai text to translate instantly. Works offline too.
🛒
Baby Supplies
Pampers, baby food, formula available at every 7-Eleven in Thailand. Don't overpack these.
